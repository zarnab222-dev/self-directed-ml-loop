# Example starter resources

A cold-start pointer, **not a curriculum**. The whole point of this loop is that
`study-scout` finds current, high-fit resources for *your* specific confusion, and
your diagnostic builds *your* phased plan. Treat everything below as a generic
default to swap out, not a canon to follow.

These are widely-recommended, mostly-free starting points for the **math
foundations** layer (linear algebra, calculus, probability/statistics) that almost
every applied-ML path shares. Nothing here is personal to any one curriculum. Once
you've run your diagnostic, let `study-scout` take over per-topic and swap in
whatever fits your gaps better.

## Math foundations

Intuition first, then formalize: build a visual picture of a mechanism before you
grind its notation.

- **[3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com/)** — *Essence of Linear Algebra* and
  *Essence of Calculus* (video, free). Best-in-class visual intuition for what
  vectors, matrices, determinants, and derivatives *mean* before the algebra.
- **[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/)** — probability & statistics, plus
  linear algebra and calculus tracks (free). Clear explanations with lots of graded
  practice; good for filling a specific gap rather than a full sit-through.
- **[MIT 18.06 — Linear Algebra (Strang)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/)**
  — full OCW course with problem sets *and solutions* (free). The keyed psets are an
  external oracle you can grade yourself against, not just watch.
- **[An Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/)** (ISL)
  — free PDF. The gentle bridge from the math to classical ML models (regression,
  resampling, regularization, trees); labs in R and Python.
- **[Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.github.io/)** (Deisenroth,
  Faisal, Ong) — free PDF. Ties linear algebra, calculus, and probability directly
  to ML; a solid "formalize it" reference after the intuition lands.
- **[Imperial College — Mathematics for ML](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/mathematics-machine-learning)**
  (Coursera) — structured linear algebra + multivariable calculus + PCA. The one
  paid item here (auditing is free); the MML book above covers the same ground for
  free if you'd rather not pay.

Budget note: everything except the Coursera specialization is free, matching this
loop's free-first default. Reach for a paid resource only when it clearly beats the
free path for your specific gap.
